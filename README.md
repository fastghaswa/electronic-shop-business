# Pixel And Profit Parade

Create a complete, playable 3D electronics shop management simulation game named "GHASWA ELECTRONIC".

GAME START:

Starting balance: ₹10,000

Starting level: 1

Starting with a small electronics shop.

One cash counter with a computer.

One small warehouse beside the shop.

SHOP MANAGEMENT

Player can walk around inside the shop.

Open and close the shop every day.

Customers enter the shop and purchase products.

Player manages the entire shop.

Add daily sales, expenses and profit reports.

PRODUCT SYSTEM
Add at least 60 electronic products:
Fans, Coolers, ACs, Refrigerators, Washing Machines, TVs, Speakers, Smartphones, Laptops, Mixers, Juicers, Geysers, Heaters, Microwaves, Chargers, Headphones, Keyboards, Mouse, Printers and many more.

BUY AND SELL

Start with ₹10,000.

Order products using the computer on the cash counter.

Buy products at wholesale prices.

Sell products at retail prices.

Earn profit and XP.

Maintain product stock.

DELIVERY VEHICLE

A delivery car or van arrives outside the shop.

It brings ordered products.

Player can unload products.

Products can be moved into the shop or warehouse.

WORKERS
Allow player to hire:

Cashier

Salesman

Stock Worker

Warehouse Manager

Delivery Worker

Workers unlock as the player's money and level increase.
Workers receive salaries and perform their assigned jobs.

WAREHOUSE

Build a separate warehouse beside the shop.

Store extra products.

Add shelves and storage racks.

Move products between shop and warehouse.

Upgrade warehouse capacity.

CUSTOM SHOP ARRANGEMENT

Player can pick up and place products anywhere.

Move and rotate products.

Arrange shelves, TVs, fans, refrigerators and coolers.

Let players design their own shop.

LEVEL SYSTEM

Start at Level 1.

Gain XP by selling products.

Unlock new products, workers and shop upgrades.

Expand into a large electronics showroom.

LEADERBOARD

Global player ranking.

Show player name, level, earnings and products sold.

Top 100 players.

Online leaderboard with database support.

GAME CAMERA

First Person Camera.

Third Person Camera.

Player can freely walk inside the shop and warehouse.

GAME DESIGN

Beautiful realistic 3D graphics.

Realistic customers and workers.

Animated delivery vehicles.

Attractive electronics showroom.

Smooth movement and controls.

Mobile and desktop support.

SAVE SYSTEM

Automatically save money, inventory, level, workers and shop layout.

Allow player to continue from saved progress.

IMPORTANT:
This must be a fully playable 3D simulation game, not a shopping website or static dashboard.
All transactions must use virtual game money.
Make all game buttons and interactions functional.
